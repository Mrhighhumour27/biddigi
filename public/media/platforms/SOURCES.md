# Original platform placement examples

Retrieved 5 November 2026. The complete per-example registry (publisher, format,
source page, original media URL, poster, dimensions and limitations) is also
exported as `SOURCES.json`. The runtime registry is `src/redesign/placementExamples.ts`.

## JioHotstar

https://ads.hotstar.com/ad-formats/ links the pre-roll and mid-roll MP4s already
stored in `../jiohotstar/`. New posters are unmodified full-frame stills at 00:05
(pre-roll) and 00:07 (mid-roll), preserving the phone frame, original advertiser
and interface. Their source names and format distinctions are retained. The
guide's duplicated pre-roll asset is not presented as a fence-ad demonstration.

## Prime Video / Amazon Ads

The interactive-video guide https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/interactive-video
provides the original `gd_Screenshot_body._TTW_.jpg` example. It shows a discovery
call to action on a streaming video ad. The original SABRE creative is preserved.
This is an Amazon streaming-TV format example; it does not establish availability
of this exact feature for every Prime Video market or booking. Restricted
channel-promotion formats are not substituted for general brand advertising.

## Sony LIV

https://advertisewithus.sonyliv.com/ currently embeds the clear public marketing
reel https://streaming.sonyliv.com/Sony_Liv_Video_Final_tv.mpd . `sony-spotlight.png`
is the full, uncropped 00:45 frame showing its own “Ads on Spotlight” Samsung
example. The source title, phone and advertising creative remain intact.
The reel uses a historical interface, identified on the page; the source link
opens the official website to view its reel. No content programme or protected
subscription stream was captured.

## YouTube / Google Ads

https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2375464?hl=en provides the original
in-stream GIF and in-feed/Shorts SVG illustrations. SVGs are stored unchanged.
The GIF is preserved as the source; its first frame is the static WebP preview,
and its original animation is encoded to MP4 for manual playback with controls.
The one-pixel MP4 pad required for even codec dimensions does not crop artwork.
No animated asset is rendered automatically, including under reduced motion.

## ZEE5

The publisher-authored March 2022 media kit hosted by The Media Ant is the
original-media URL in the registry. Page 19 is rendered in full, uncropped, at
1440×810, preserving its heading, ad-format labels and advertiser examples.
It shows video inventory, display banners and native placements. It is explicitly
labelled as a historical interface, not represented as a current UI capture.

The current official format listing at
https://adsmanagerhelp.zee5.com/portal/en/kb/articles/ad-formats confirms video,
medium rectangle, native, leaderboard and mobile banner formats. The 300×250
medium rectangle is described from that document; the gallery does not mislabel
the kit's horizontal banner screenshot as that specific unit.

These are publisher examples, not claims of Biddigi-produced customer work.
Biddigi's direct execution copy follows the owner's explicit confirmation;
no official-partner badge is added.
